Note: 3 days, about 1:30 each.

ROUTINE: Chest and biceps @ mon 18:00
Elliptical 10 min level 6

Cable fly 3x12-15 last to failure rest 1:00
- Elbows slightly bent and still.

Hammer curl (dumbbells) 3x12 rest 1:00
